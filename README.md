SUN-SEARCHERS AI
"Hello, welcome to Sun-Searchers, my name is Carol. Can I help you?"

The Backstory

While walking to work, I remembered the classic Little Britain sketches and asked myself: what would Carol Beer be like in the world of AI?

Instead of an overly eager assistant that instantly answers everything, I imagined Carol as a deadpan bureaucratic AI agent who refuses your prompt a random number of times before finally sighing and letting Gemini actually help you.

Features

Unpredictable Stubbornness: Randomly rejects your inquiry (up to 10 times depending on her mood) before saying "Computer says yes."

Deadpan Quirks & Retro SFX: Built-in Web Audio API mechanical keystrokes, rejection buzzers, and Carol's dry double-cough—no external audio files needed.

Real Gemini AI Handoff: Once you wear down her patience, she reluctantly forwards your request to Gemini for a genuinely helpful response.
